Complete Heating & Cooling Services for Idaho Falls Properties

We service every type of heating and cooling equipment you'll find in local homes and businesses, from central air conditioners and gas furnaces to heat pumps and ductless mini-splits. It makes no difference whether the building is a single-family house, a condo, or a busy office. Our technicians understand the local weather, the way homes here were built, and the code requirements that apply to new installations, which pays off in every diagnosis.

Most homeowners we work with need one of three things: a repair, a replacement, or a seasonal tune-up. Repairs are usually straightforward: a burned contactor, a dirty flame sensor, a refrigerant leak, or a seized blower motor. When we replace a system, it gets a proper load calculation, not a guess based on the old unit's tonnage, which keeps temperatures even and stops the new equipment from short-cycling itself to an early death. Tune-ups include coil cleaning, a refrigerant charge check, safety control testing, and a static pressure reading, and nothing does more to extend the life of your system.

Commercial work is a big part of what we do as well. We service rooftop units, split systems, and ventilation equipment for restaurants, retail stores, offices, and multi-unit buildings, and we know that a day without cooling hurts the business. To keep disruptions short, we carry common parts on every truck, diagnose quickly, and plan larger jobs around your operating hours whenever possible.

Quick Heating and Cooling Help in Idaho Falls

Heating and cooling failures rarely wait for a convenient day, which is why we offer fast, flexible scheduling for most situations. When you call, you'll speak with a real person who will get a technician dispatched to your property without delay. Our typical arrival window in the area is usually within a day for urgent calls, and a dead AC in a heat wave or a furnace out on a freezing night is exactly the kind of call we move to the front of the line.

Here's what we see most often when something breaks at home: an air conditioner blowing warm air, a condenser that hums but won't start, a furnace that ignites and shuts off, a breaker that keeps tripping, an evaporator coil that has iced over, a condensate drain overflowing into the furnace cabinet, or a thermostat that has gone blank. Nearly all of these trace back to parts already on our trucks: capacitors, contactors, igniters, flame sensors, universal control boards, and standard fittings. You'll see the price up front, and that's the price you pay.

A little caution while you wait for us can prevent a much larger repair. An iced-over AC should be turned off at the thermostat with the fan set to "on", which lets the coil thaw faster. Running it frozen risks an overflowing drain pan and a damaged compressor. A short-cycling furnace, or any burning smell, means it's time to shut the unit off at its service switch and call us immediately.

Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Idaho Falls

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep shrubs and grass trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and schedule a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

How old is your system? Its age tells you a lot. Most well-maintained air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that point and is also showing rising bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repeated repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than a rushed emergency install when the first heat wave hits.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor ignored for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
